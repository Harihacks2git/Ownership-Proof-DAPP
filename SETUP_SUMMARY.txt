================================================================================
  🐛 TROUBLESHOOTING
================================================================================

IPFS NOT CONNECTING:
   → Run: ipfs daemon
   → Check: http://127.0.0.1:5001/webui

CONTRACT NOT CONNECTING:
   → Restart Hardhat node
   → Redeploy contract
   → Update CONTRACT_ADDR in configs

METAMASK ERRORS:
   → Settings → Advanced → Clear activity data
   → Restart Hardhat and redeploy

PORT ALREADY IN USE:
   → ./start-services.sh (option 8 to stop all)
   → Or manually kill: lsof -ti:<port> | xargs kill -9

For detailed troubleshooting, see SETUP.md
